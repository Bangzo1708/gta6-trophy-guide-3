    Roadmap to the platinum

    One playthrough, no difficulty settings, no mission medals and no mission replay (PowerPyx). Free roam continues after the story, so almost everything can be cleaned up at the end — except the one missable meditation in Step 1.

    Step 1 · about 60–80 hours

    Story & side strands

    Play the story at your own pace. Along the way, do these because they all count later:

    • Do the Chapter 1 meditation at Jason’s house the morning after Down to Earth — the only possibly missable trophy.
    • Take optional missions as they appear. 100% needs any 100 of them, out of several hundred.
    • Scan every new vehicle with the WAiNK app. 100% needs 200 of 277 base models.
    • Study new animals through the binoculars. 100% needs 100 of 255. You do not have to hunt them.
    • Start the stranger strands when they call or appear — Andy (Ch. 3, Lucia only), Billy (Ch. 2), Randy (Ch. 3), Tyrone (Ch. 3, Jason only), Kenton (treasure diving, 7 shipwrecks).
    • Turn on progress notifications in the UI settings, so you can see what counts.

    Trophies here: all 12 story trophies, Positive Mental Attitude, Master of None, Baited My Tackle, Stick Fiddler, Hot Together, Retail Therapy, Gunslinger, Sneakthief, A Classic Blitz Play.

    Step 2 · about 100 hours

    The 100% checklist & challenges

    The checklist appears after the story, under Progress, then Stats, then 100% Completion. Progress made during the story already counts, and PowerPyx says nothing on it is missable.

    PowerPyx calls the checklist lenient — closer to 60% true completion. The table below is the whole of it. The gold A Long Road is the hardest trophy in the game.

    Trophies here: A Long Road, Cunning Stunts, The Shootist, Top Fun, Earn Cash Not Kudos, Don’t Touch My Deck, You WAiNKer!, One Last Thing, Whip It Good, Maximum Withdrawal, The Goodtime State, Pest Control, Fair Game, Dime on the Dollar, Treat Yo Self, Little Boxes, On the Spot.

    Step 3 · about 10 hours

    Strangers & Freaks + cleanup

    Most trophies should be done by now. PowerPyx says Strangers & Freaks is not part of 100%, so you may still need those 8 strands — finish any stranger questline you have left, sweep the remaining unconfirmed trophies (In The Beginning…, Bad Luck, I Guess, Ten Pin) once guides confirm them, and the platinum follows.

    Trophies here: Strangers & Freaks, the 5 stranger-strand finals (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone), Vice Got a Price! (platinum).

    Missables & saves

    Good news first: only one trophy may be missable (PowerPyx; Push Square lists none). Everything else can be finished in free roam after the story.

    The one missable

    Positive Mental Attitude

    Bronze · Meditate at every spot across Leonida · 18 spots in 10 locations

    The first meditation is offered in Chapter 1: the morning after the mission Down to Earth, Lucia says she is going to meditate and walks out to the balcony at Jason’s house. Follow her and accept the optional meditation. It is gone after Chapter 1.

    PowerPyx reports that it raises the meditation counter by one, but it is not marked as a meditation spot like the other 18 — so it is unclear whether the trophy needs it. PowerPyx had done it when the trophy unlocked. Do it when it comes up. It takes a minute and removes the risk.

    Save strategy: keep a manual save from the start of Chapter 1 until this trophy pops. The game has 15 manual save slots plus 1 autosave.

    Save strategy

    Keep your run safe

    • 15 manual slots + 1 autosave. Missions cannot be replayed, so PowerPyx suggests keeping a save at the start and end of each chapter, then rotating the rest during free roam.
    • Chapter 1 start save: keep it until Positive Mental Attitude pops — it is your only way back if you skip the balcony meditation.
    • Before big choices: save before story choices (Actions Speak Louder counts 500 profile-changing acts; choices feed it either way).
    • Cheats disable trophies — and stop the game saving. Keep them off the file you use for your platinum run.

    Looks missable, but is not

    Do not worry about these

    • Optional missions that disappear after a while (black icons with pink letters). There are far more than you need.
    • Quest givers who go away for part of the story — Kenton for one. All trophy quest givers come back and stay after the story.
    • Fences who leave for good. None of them has a trophy quest.
    • Magazine collectibles in story buildings that close off. They also spawn elsewhere.
    • Timed quests (hourglass icon). None is tied to a trophy.
    • The Jason–Lucia relationship. It does not matter for trophies.
